Trove turns your screenshot folder into a searchable library. It reads the text in each screenshot on your phone, sorts them into categories, and lets you search by any word you remember seeing.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Full-text search across everything written in your screenshots</li>
<li>Automatic categories such as receipts, code, and conversations</li>
<li>Collections, tags, notes, and favorites</li>
<li>Tap a link, email address, or phone number found in a screenshot to open it</li>
<li>Exact-duplicate finder. Deletion always asks first through Android's own dialog</li>
<li>Light, dark, and dynamic color themes</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
No account, no ads, no internet permission. Text recognition and sorting run on your phone. Your screenshot files are never copied or moved.
